How to Apply

Simple steps to join a private Technical Secondary School

1

Create an Account

Register as Student/Visitor or as Director Education Officer (DEO).

2

Browse & Choose a School

Filter by district or search programmes.

3

Submit Application

Select programme, level and payment method.

4

Complete Payment

Use MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, Bank or Card.

5

Track Status

View Pending → Under Review → Approved / Rejected.

Payment Methods

Flexible and secure ways to pay

📱

MTN Mobile Money

Dial *182# or use MoMo App with the generated reference.

📱

Airtel Money

Use Airtel Money app or USSD with your unique reference.

🏦

Bank Transfer

Transfer to the school account and note the transaction ID.

💳

Visa / Mastercard

Card payments are recorded with a generated reference.

How payment works: When you apply, you receive a unique payment reference. After you pay, mark I have paid. Only the Admin verifies and marks the payment as Paid.
